How buying and using Penstock works: the trial, the plans, cancelling, refunds and support. The licence to the software itself is the end user licence.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Short version

  • Penstock is free to use, with no time limit. Pro and Team are paid subscriptions you can cancel at any time.
  • Every new machine starts with a 30-day Pro trial. No card is needed.
  • Your first payment is refunded in full on request within 30 days. A renewal within 14 days.
  • Polar processes payments and taxes. We do not see your card.
  • Your diagrams and files are yours.

2. The free product

The features listed as Free on the pricing page are available without payment and with no time limit.

3. Trial

Every machine starts with 30 days of Pro, with no sign-up and no card. The trial is per machine and the licence service keeps its start date. A pipeline, meaning a run where the environment variable CI is set, gets no trial.

4. Plans, prices and tax

  • Pro costs $9 a month or $90 a year. Team costs $29 a seat a month or $290 a seat a year. Prices are in US dollars. The price you pay is the one shown at checkout.
  • A subscription renews at the end of each period until you cancel.
  • Polar is the merchant of record. It calculates and collects VAT or sales tax where it applies and issues receipts and invoices.

5. Cancelling

Cancel at any time from the link in your receipt or from Polar's customer portal. You keep the paid features until the end of the period you paid for. After that your machines return to the Free plan, and your files are not touched.

6. Refunds

Write to oleksandr.havrysh5@gmail.com and ask. Your first payment for a plan is refunded in full if you ask within 30 days of it. A renewal is refunded in full if you ask within 14 days of the charge. Refunds are processed through Polar to the payment method you used.

7. Licence keys, machines and seats

One key covers three machines at once, for Pro and for each Team seat. You can free a place by signing out on a machine, or by picking a machine to free when you sign in on another. Team seats are given out in Polar's customer portal. A Team key for pipelines takes no machine's place. Keep your key to yourself and to the people of your organisation. How it works is described on Buying and signing in.

8. Students, teachers and open-source maintainers

Students, teachers and open-source maintainers can get Pro for free. We decide who qualifies and may ask for proof. Write to oleksandr.havrysh5@gmail.com.

9. Using Penstock

The EULA sets what you may and may not do with the software. Using Penstock to edit and review your own diagrams is always permitted.

10. Your content

Your BPMN, DMN and form files are yours. Penstock does not send them to us, and we claim no rights in them.
