Collapsed sub-process

compat/collapsed-subprocess · Engine compatibility · Error: the diagram or the process is wrong

Why it matters

This version of Camunda 8 cannot run a sub-process drawn collapsed on its own diagram, because it has no plane to read it from.

How to fix it

Expand the sub-process, or raise the platform version of the diagram.

In penstock check and in the editor the rule is called compat/collapsed-subprocess. In .bpmnlintrc it is turned off as camunda-compat/collapsed-subprocess or as compat/collapsed-subprocess.
