FEEL expression that is not valid

compat/feel · Engine compatibility · Error: the diagram or the process is wrong

Why it matters

An expression the engine cannot parse fails as soon as the process reaches it, and stops the instance with an incident.

How to fix it

Correct the expression named in the finding; the editor of the field shows where it goes wrong.

In penstock check and in the editor the rule is called compat/feel. In .bpmnlintrc it is turned off as camunda-compat/feel or as compat/feel.
