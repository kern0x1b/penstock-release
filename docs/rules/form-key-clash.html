Two fields write the same variable

form/key-clash · Forms · Error: the diagram or the process is wrong

Why it matters

Tasklist and the form editor refuse to open a form in which two fields, or a field and a group, use the same key.

How to fix it

Give each field its own key.

In penstock check and in the editor the rule is called KEY-CLASH.
