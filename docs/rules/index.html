Engine compatibility

What the engine the diagram is written for cannot run: a missing job type, an element or an expression the chosen version does not support. They run on Camunda 7 and Camunda 8 diagrams, in the editor and in penstock check, against the version in the file's modeler:executionPlatformVersion, or the newest one without it. Operaton and CIB seven are checked as Camunda 7. A finding is called compat/<rule> here and in penstock check; a rule is turned off in .bpmnlintrc as camunda-compat/<rule> or, the same, compat/<rule>.
