Timer that is not valid

compat/timer · Engine compatibility · Error: the diagram or the process is wrong

Why it matters

A timer with a date, duration or cycle the engine cannot read fails when the process is deployed or reached.

How to fix it

Write the timer as an ISO 8601 date, duration or cycle, or as an expression.

In penstock check and in the editor the rule is called compat/timer. In .bpmnlintrc it is turned off as camunda-compat/timer or as compat/timer.
