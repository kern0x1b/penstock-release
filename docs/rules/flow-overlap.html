Two ways out of a decision can both hold

flow/overlap · Process flow · Error: the diagram or the process is wrong

Why it matters

When two conditions of an exclusive gateway can both be true the engine takes whichever it checks first, and which one that is depends on the order in the file.

How to fix it

Make the conditions exclusive, or turn one of them into the default flow.

In penstock check and in the editor the rule is called OVERLAP.
