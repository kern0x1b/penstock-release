Quick start in JetBrains

From nothing installed to a checked, deployed process in a JetBrains IDE, on one screen.

Free Any JetBrains IDE from 2023.1

  1. Install. Open Settings | Plugins | Marketplace, search for Penstock and press Install, or open the Marketplace page. Restart the IDE if it asks.
  2. Write the example. Open an empty project and open the Penstock tool window. Press Camunda 8 order process or Camunda 7 order process and confirm with Create. Or open a .bpmn file of your own.
  3. Open the diagram. Double-click order-process.bpmn. The BPMN Editor and Text tabs at the foot switch between the picture and the XML.
  4. Check it. Press Check on the editor's toolbar. A card lists what is wrong. A fresh example has nothing wrong.
  5. Deploy it. Press Deploy. The first time, it asks for the engine address. Enter it, save, and deploy again.

What you should see

  • The diagram in the editor, with the palette on the left and the properties of the selected element on the right.
  • The licence status in the status bar: every new machine starts with 30 days of Pro.
  • After Deploy, the process on your engine. The Monitor tab shows its instances.
