Pricing For teams

Penstock Pro, free for education and open source

Students, teachers and maintainers of open-source projects get Penstock Pro at no cost. You write one e-mail, and the owner sends you a key by hand.

Who qualifies

  • Students with an address at their school, college or university.
  • Teachers with an address at their school, college or university.
  • Maintainers of open-source projects: people who look after a public repository under an open-source licence.

What you get

Penstock Pro, free, with everything Pro includes on your own machines: auto layout, flow analysis, code links, scenarios and test-case runs, Camunda 7 to Camunda 8 conversion, and sending messages in the AI chat. See Pricing for the full list. The key works in the JetBrains plugin, the VS Code extension, the command line and the MCP server, on up to three machines.

The key is renewed every year while you are still eligible. Penstock Free, with no time limit, stays as it is for everyone.

Pro on your machine does not cover a pipeline. A pipeline needs Team, which is not part of this offer.

How to apply

  1. Write an e-mail to oleksandr.havrysh5@gmail.com.
  2. Use this subject line: Penstock Pro for education
  3. Add one line of proof. For a student or a teacher, your school address, written from that address or quoted in the e-mail. For a maintainer, the URL of the repository.

Each application is read by a person. When it is approved, you get a Pro licence key by e-mail, and you enter it as described in Buying and signing in. If it is declined, the answer says why. A year later, write again with the same subject if you still qualify.

Nothing is sold or shared

Your e-mail address and your proof are used only to decide on your application and to issue the key. Nothing is sold or shared. See the privacy policy for what happens to the e-mail afterwards.
