If you used Camunda BPMN Editor before

Penstock is the same plugin under a new name. It updated in place, so nothing needed to be reinstalled. The version number jumped from 2.4.1 to 26.9.0 because releases are now numbered by the calendar: 26.10.2 is the second release of October 2026.

What the plugin did then is still free: the BPMN, DMN and form editors, the properties panels, element templates, token simulation, bpmnlint, the check, deploy, Monitor, export and the tool window. Everything new since 2.4.1 that only Penstock does, such as flow analysis, code links, scenarios and the AI chat, is Pro.

One thing did move. Release 2.4.1 showed the two versions of a diagram side by side in the IDE's compare window for free. That visual diagram diff is part of Pro now. The text diff of the XML is the IDE's own and is not affected.

What happens when the trial ends

  • The machine goes back to Free. The editors and everything on the Free list keep working.
  • A Pro feature shows a short message that says what it does and offers to buy or to sign in. Nothing is deleted and no file is changed.
  • The trial is per machine. The licence service keeps its start date, so reinstalling does not start a second one.
  • A pipeline gets no trial: a run where CI is set is Free unless it has a Team key.

Questions

How does the trial work?

Every machine starts with 30 days of Pro. There is nothing to sign up for and no card is asked. The licence status of your tool shows how many days are left. After day 30 the machine is on Free, and a Pro feature asks you to buy when you reach it.

What happens to my work after the trial?

Nothing. Diagrams are your own files in your project. Pro features stop running, and the files stay as they are.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Write to {{contact}} within {{config:refund.firstPaymentDays}} days of your first payment and it is refunded in full. A renewal can be refunded within {{config:refund.renewalDays}} days of the charge. Polar processes the refund.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Use the link in the receipt Polar sent you, or sign in to Polar's customer portal with the e-mail you bought with. You keep the paid features until the end of the period you paid for.

What about VAT and sales tax?

Polar is the merchant of record. It calculates and collects VAT or sales tax where it applies, and the checkout shows what you pay before you pay it.

Do I get an invoice?

Yes. Polar issues the receipt and the invoice, and handles them for you. They are available through the link in the receipt and Polar's customer portal.

How do Team seats work?

Team is priced per seat, and you choose the number of seats at checkout. You then give the seats to people in Polar's customer portal, by e-mail. Each person who accepts a seat gets a key of their own for three machines. A pipeline uses a Team key stored as a secret and takes no machine's place.

How many machines does one licence cover?

One key covers three machines at once, for Pro and for each Team seat. It works in the JetBrains plugin, the VS Code extension and the command line. You can free a place by signing in on the machine list.

Do students, teachers and open-source maintainers pay?

They can get Pro for free. The application link is not published on the site yet, so write to {{contact}} and say which group you are in, or open an issue on the support tracker.

Does the licence need an internet connection?

The licence is a signed token that Penstock checks offline. It is renewed once a day when the machine is online, and a valid token keeps working for up to 30 days without a connection. Buying, signing in and starting a trial need the licence service once.

What does the licence service receive?

The licence, a one-way hash of the machine's id and the host name as a label. It also sees the address a request comes from. Never a diagram. The full list is in the privacy policy.

Which currency do you charge in?

Prices are in US dollars. Your bank converts them if your card is in another currency.
